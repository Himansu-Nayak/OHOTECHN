# OHO TECH Domain Glossary

This glossary defines canonical business and technical terminology used across the OHO TECH platform (Next.js frontend, Spring Boot backend, and PostgreSQL database). All specifications, code, and documentation must adhere to these definitions.

---

### Core Business & Commerce Entities

- **Order**: A commercial transaction initiated by a customer purchasing digital goods, turnkey software, or developer services. Tracks amounts, customer contact details, fulfillment state, and lifecycle states (`CREATED`, `PENDING`, `COMPLETED`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`).
- **TurnkeyProduct**: A pre-built, ready-to-deploy software system, e-commerce template, SaaS starter, or developer tool catalogued and sold on OHO TECH.
- **Entitlement**: The digital right granted to an authenticated customer upon verified payment completion, including download permissions, repository invitations, or SaaS tier access.
- **LicenseKey**: A unique alphanumeric token generated and issued to a customer upon successful payment to unlock or activate licensed software.

---

### Payment Infrastructure & Routing

- **PaymentGateway**: An external payment processing provider integrated into OHO TECH:
  - `RAZORPAY`: Razorpay Standard Checkout & Webhooks.
  - `CASHFREE`: Cashfree Payment Gateway & Webhook callbacks.
  - `UPI_HOST` (Self-Hosted UPI / UPIHost): OHO TECH's zero-fee, direct-to-bank UPI payment infrastructure utilizing dynamic QR codes and real-time bank credit alerts.
  - `DIRECT_UPI`: Manual static QR payment flow requiring manual transaction reference verification.
  - `COD`: Cash On Delivery / Offline payment arrangement.
  - `DEMO`: Sandbox/test payment mode restricted to non-production environments.
- **PaymentSlotLease**: An in-memory or database-backed lease that temporarily locks a specific order, payment session, and unique paise offset or dynamic reference to eliminate collision risks during concurrent UPI payments.
- **ObserverDevice**: A secured, authorized Android hardware device running the OHO TECH UPI Observer client app, which forwards verified bank SMS and push notification alerts to the backend via signed HTTP/WebSocket endpoints.
- **BankCreditAlert**: A record of funds received in the merchant's bank account, ingested from observer devices or bank webhooks, containing amount, bank reference, timestamp, and RRN/UTR.
- **RRN (Retrieval Reference Number) / UTR (Unique Transaction Reference)**: The unique 12-digit transaction reference number generated by the NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) banking switch for every UPI transfer.
- **VPA (Virtual Payment Address)**: The UPI ID handle (e.g. `merchant@okhdfcbank`) receiving the incoming payment transfer.
- **Reconciliation**: The automated and manual process of auditing gateway statements, bank credit alerts, and database transaction logs to detect discrepancies, missing orders, or unfulfilled settlements.

---

### Security & Integrity Concepts

- **RRN Deduplication**: Strict database-level and application-level checks ensuring no single 12-digit RRN can ever be claimed, matched, or fulfilled for more than one order.
- **Lease Expiry**: The timeout mechanism (typically 10-15 minutes) after which an uncompleted payment lease releases its reserved slot.
- **Signature Verification**: Cryptographic validation of webhook payloads (HMAC-SHA256) ensuring events originate from authorized payment gateways.
